About Me

Hi, I’m Helly — an IT student by degree, but an artist at heart.

I primarily work with acrylics and sketches, exploring different mediums and techniques along the way. My art is deeply inspired by divine figures, whose presence and symbolism influence much of what I create.

For me, art is not about perfection — it’s about feeling, growth, and honesty. I believe in creating slowly, embracing imperfections, and letting each piece evolve naturally.

Every artwork I make is a reflection of emotion, spirituality, and a quiet connection between the art and the viewer.
